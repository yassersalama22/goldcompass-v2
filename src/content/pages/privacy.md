## In short

GoldCompass has no user accounts and does not sell your data. We collect your email address only if you subscribe to the newsletter, and we measure how the site is used so we can improve it and understand which of our advertising works. The calculators run in your browser.

## Newsletter

If you subscribe, we collect your email address, the date, and which page you subscribed from. Your address is stored by Buttondown, the email service that sends the newsletter. We also pass your IP address to Buttondown when you sign up, so it can detect and block abusive signups.

We use double opt-in: nothing is sent to you beyond a single confirmation email until you confirm. Every newsletter contains an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing stops all further emails.

## Bot protection

The subscribe form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that a signup comes from a person rather than an automated script. To do that, Cloudflare processes technical signals from your browser. To limit abuse, our server also keeps your IP address in memory for a few minutes after a signup attempt; it is not written to a database.

## Analytics

We use two analytics services:

- **Cloudflare Web Analytics** counts page views in aggregate. It sets no cookies and does not identify individual visitors.
- **Google Analytics** tells us which pages are read, how visitors arrive (for example, from a search engine or an advertisement), and whether they subscribe. It uses cookies (named `_ga` and `_ga_…`) to recognise a returning browser, and records the page address, the referring page, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address.

The calculators mirror their inputs into the page address so that a result can be shared. We remove those inputs before the address is sent to Google Analytics, so your figures are not recorded there.

We never send your email address or name to Google Analytics.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Google Analytics runs without cookies and nothing is stored on your device.

## Advertising

We advertise GoldCompass on Google Ads. When you arrive by clicking one of our ads, Google adds a click identifier to the page address, and Google Analytics uses it, together with cookies (named `_gcl_…`), to tell us which ads brought you to the site and how ad visitors use it, for example which pages they read and whether they subscribe. This is how we judge which ads are worth paying for.

We do not use your visits to build advertising audiences, we do not show you personalised ads based on our site, and there is no other advertising on GoldCompass.

## Calculators

The gold calculators run entirely in your browser. Your inputs are not sent to our server, except as part of the page address when you load or share a calculator link.

## Preferences stored on your device

If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is kept in your browser’s local storage so the site remembers it. A short-lived marker in session storage prevents a newsletter confirmation from being counted twice. Neither is sent to us.

## Who processes your data

Your data is processed on our behalf by the providers named above: Buttondown (newsletter), Cloudflare (website delivery, bot protection, and Web Analytics), Google (Analytics and Ads), and Amazon Web Services, which hosts our server. These providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data.

## Your choices

- Unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any email.
- Block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site works without them.
- Opt out of Google Analytics on all websites with Google’s [browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- Manage how Google uses data for advertising in your [Google ad settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
